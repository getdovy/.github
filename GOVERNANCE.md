# Governance

## Maintainers

Maintainers are the members of the `maintainers` team and the owners of
the getdovy organisation. Every maintainer has the same rights, the
project's founder included. No one holds a right the others lack.

Maintainers review and merge pull requests, approve releases, and decide
what belongs in the project.

## Releases

Releases are signed by the project's own keys, which no person holds:
the release workflow signs, once a maintainer other than the one who
merged approves. A release needs two people, never one.

## Decisions

Most decisions are made in review. When maintainers disagree, they vote:
a decision passes with a majority of all maintainers. A tie leaves things
as they are.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors with a record of accepted pull requests and helpful reviews
may be proposed by any maintainer, or ask to be. They join by a vote of
the maintainers.

## Leaving

- Any maintainer may step down at any time; nothing needs handing over.
- A maintainer inactive for six months becomes a former maintainer, and
  may come back by asking.
- A maintainer, the founder included, can be removed by a vote of the
  other maintainers.

Leaving removes the maintainer's team membership and organisation rights.
Shared secrets the maintainer knew are replaced.

## Changes

This document changes by a vote of the maintainers, like any other
decision.
